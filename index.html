🔥 Free Fire Tips & Tricks

Sensitivity Adjustment

Start from the Sensitivity Finder above, then adjust in small ±5 increments per scope level until your crosshair lands where you expect it to, not past it.

HUD Positioning

Keep fire, jump, and crouch buttons within natural thumb reach without stretching — a cramped HUD costs reaction time more than low sensitivity ever does.

Drag Technique

Practice a short, controlled upward drag rather than a long swipe — it's faster to repeat and easier to stop exactly on target.

Recoil Control

Learn each gun's pattern in the practice range first; recoil control is muscle memory, not a sensitivity setting.

Crosshair Placement

Pre-aim at head height around corners and doorways so your first shot on contact is already close to a headshot.

Movement Practice

Drill strafing while shooting in the training range — standing still is the single biggest reason for missed close-range fights.

Close-Range Aim

Lower your general/red-dot sensitivity slightly if you consistently overshoot close targets; it's more common than being too slow.

Long-Range Aim

Use small, deliberate drag adjustments at 2x/4x/sniper zoom — large sweeps at high zoom overshoot far more than they correct.

Reaction Training

The Reaction Test and Quick Tap games in the Gaming Zone are genuinely useful daily warm-ups, not just for fun.

Device Performance

Close background apps before playing, and enable any "Game Mode"/performance mode your device offers to reduce frame drops mid-fight.

FPS Stability

A stable 40fps beats a spiky 60fps for aim consistency — check in-game graphics settings if your frame rate fluctuates a lot.

Touch Response

Remove any screen protector with poor touch response, and clean your screen — both measurably affect drag precision.
